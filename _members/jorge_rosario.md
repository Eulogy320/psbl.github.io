Microbiologist with expertise in molecular plant science and bioinformatics. I specialize in the evolutionary analysis of nutrient-sensing pathways, specifically nitrate perception, using the bryophyte model Marchantia polymorpha. My professional experience includes the study of Plant Growth-Promoting Bacteria (PGPB) and the application of computational methods to decode complex evolutionary processes in terrestrial vegetation.
