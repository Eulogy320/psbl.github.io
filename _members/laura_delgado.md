My research focuses on how plants respond to fluctuations in nitrogen availability and how previous nitrogen conditions shape future responses. I integrate morphological, physiological, and bioinformatic approaches to investigate the mechanisms underlying plant responses to temporal changes in nitrate availability, with particular emphasis on root system responses. Using Arabidopsis thaliana, my work aims to uncover how plants sense, use, and respond to dynamic nutritional environments.
