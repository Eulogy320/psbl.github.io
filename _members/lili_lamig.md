Dr. Liliana Lamig is a biochemist at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, where she also obtained her PhD in Biological Sciences. Her research focuses on elucidating the cellular and molecular mechanisms by which nitrate regulates the cell cycle in leaf epidermal cells through hormonal signals in the model plant Arabidopsis thaliana. Her research integrates cellular biology approaches, including confocal microscopy and flow cytometry, together with genetic tools such as fluorescent reporter lines and mutant backgrounds.
